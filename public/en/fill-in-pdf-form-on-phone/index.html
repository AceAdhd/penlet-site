How do I fill in a PDF form on my phone?

Open the form in Penlet and tap the fields. If the PDF has fillable fields, you type straight into them and tick the boxes. If the form is flat, with no fields to tap, Penlet finds the boxes and lines itself, so you fill it in the same way. When you’re done, tap “Save” to get a filled-in copy.

Forms with fillable fields

Many PDF forms from councils, schools and companies have real form fields. You fill them in directly in Penlet: text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons and lists. The values are written into the fields of the copy you save, so the recipient sees them in any PDF reader.

Forms with no fields

A printed form turned into a PDF often has only lines and boxes, no fields. Penlet looks for the boxes and writing lines on the page and turns them into fields you can tap, named after the label next to them. It works on paper forms you have photographed too.

  • If Penlet misses a field, choose “Text”, “Cross”, “Check” or “Date” and tap where it should go.
  • If it found something that isn’t a field, press and hold it to remove it. “Undo” brings it back.
  • When you save, the fields become real form fields in the copy.

Dates, crosses and ticks

You pick a date in a calendar, or type it yourself. It is written the way your phone writes dates. Crosses and ticks go anywhere, and everything you place can be moved, resized and deleted afterwards.

Finish later

Your changes are kept as a draft on the phone as you go. If you close the app halfway, the form waits under “Documents” until you’re done.

Finally: sign and send

If the form needs a signature, add it in the same go – see how to sign a PDF on your phone. Then tap “Save”, and “Share” to send the copy.

Questions and answers

My form has no fields to tap. Can I still fill it in?

Yes. Penlet finds the boxes and lines in a printed form by itself, so you just tap them and type. It works on forms you have photographed too. If it misses a field, tap where the text should go and type.

Will the recipient see what I filled in?

Yes. Penlet saves a new copy of the PDF with the values in the form fields, so they show in any PDF reader. The original is not changed.

Can I finish filling it in later?

Yes. Your changes are kept as a draft on the phone, and the form waits under “Documents” until you’re done.
