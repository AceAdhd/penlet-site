Privacy policy for Penlet

Last updated 28 October 2026

Penlet is an app for reading, filling in, signing and organising PDF documents on your phone. It is made by Trolda AS, Norway.

In short:

  • Everything happens on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded anywhere.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising.
  • The app does not collect any information about you and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It does not even request internet access.
  • The purchase that unlocks the app goes through Google Play. We never receive your card details.

Who we are

Penlet is made by Trolda AS in Norway. For privacy questions, email support@penlet.app.

What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps the following locally, in its own private storage. Other apps cannot access it, and we never receive it.

WhatWhy
DraftsDocuments you have opened, with your changes, so you can continue later. You remove a draft from the list on the home screen.
Your signatureIf you draw or photograph a signature, it is stored on the phone so you do not have to make it again.
SettingsFor example the folder you last saved to, and whether tips are shown.
Trial and purchaseWhether you have bought Penlet, and which documents you saved or shared while trying the app for free (only an internal key per document, not its name or content). The trial is also kept in Google’s Block Store (Google Play services), so it does not start over if the app is reinstalled, and it can follow your Google account to a new phone, encrypted.

Apart from the trial, none of this is included in Google’s backup or transferred to a new phone, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

When you save a PDF, it is written where you choose (Downloads or another folder). The original file is never modified; the app always creates a new copy.

Camera and photos

When you take a picture of a document or a signature, the app uses your phone’s camera app or photo picker. The app does not request its own access to the camera or your photos; it only receives the picture you take or choose. The pictures are processed on the phone and deleted from the app’s cache right after they have been read.

Text recognition

To make photographed documents searchable and to find the fields in them, the app uses the text recognition in Google Play services (ML Kit) on Android. Recognition runs on the phone; the images are not sent to Google. The first time, Google Play services may download the language model, which can require a network connection. Google Play services is Google’s software and is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Penlet can be tried for free and is unlocked with a single purchase. The purchase is made in Google Play, and Google handles the payment under its own terms and Google's privacy policy. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether the purchase has been made; it sends nothing about you to us. Google gives us the order information we need for accounting (for example order number, product, price and country), but never your card details.

Password-protected PDFs

The password you enter to open a protected PDF is held in memory only while the document is open. It is not stored.

Sharing

When you use “Share”, you hand the file to the app you choose (email, messaging and so on). What that app does with the file is governed by that app’s terms. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share diagnostic data with Google on your phone, Google Play may give us anonymous reports about crashes and performance. They do not contain your documents or anything you have entered.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply. If you sign up as a tester, we add your email address to the tester list in Google Play Console so you get access to the test. We delete the email and remove you from the tester list when you ask, or when we no longer need it.

The website penlet.app

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party content. The fonts are served from our own server. The provider hosting the site may record technical details such as your IP address in server logs, for operation and security.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete information we hold about you. Since the app sends nothing to us, we usually only have emails you have sent, and the order information Google gives us when you buy the app. If you believe we process data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. The app will still not collect any information unless it is stated here.
