How do I turn a paper document into a PDF with my phone?

Tap “Take photo of document” in Penlet and take a picture of the sheet. Penlet finds the edges of the sheet, straightens it and cleans up the background. If the document has more pages, tap “Take photo of next page”. When you’re done, you get one PDF with all the pages, with text you can search.

Step by step

  1. Tap “Take photo of document” on Penlet’s front page. Choose “Take photo”, or “From photos” if you already took the picture.
  2. Check the corners. Penlet finds the sheet by itself. If something is off, drag the corners. “Rotate” turns the page.
  3. Choose a look: “Document”, “Grayscale”, “Black & white” or “Photo”. Tap “Use”.
  4. More pages? Tap “Take photo of next page” and carry on. Tap “Done” at the end.

The pages become A4, portrait or landscape to match the sheet. Skewed text is straightened, and the background around the sheet is cut away.

Text you can search

Penlet reads the text in the picture and places it invisibly underneath, so you can search the PDF and copy from it. Text recognition runs on the phone in Google Play services. The first time, Google may download the language model, and that needs internet.

Fill in and sign right away

If it was a form, Penlet finds the boxes and writing lines in the picture, so you can fill it in and sign it before you send it back.

Forgot a page?

Open “Pages” in the document and tap “Take photos of more pages”. There you can also move, rotate and delete pages.

Questions and answers

Is the PDF searchable?

Yes. Penlet reads the text in the picture and places it invisibly under the page, so you can search the document. Text recognition runs on the phone.

Are the photos sent to a server?

No. The photos are processed on the phone and deleted from the app’s cache right after they are read. Text recognition runs on the phone in Google Play services, and the photos are not sent to Google.

Can I use photos I have already taken?

Yes. Choose “From photos” instead of “Take photo”, and the picture is processed the same way.
