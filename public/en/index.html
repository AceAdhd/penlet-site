Everything a form needs. Nothing more.

Big buttons with labels and no menus to dig through. Made so anyone gets it right the first time.

  • My details

    Enter your name, address, postcode, phone and email once, then add them with one tap. Optional, and only on your phone.

  • Date with a calendar

    Today’s date is suggested. Tap to choose another day in the month view.

  • Cross and tick

    Tap the boxes you want to mark, with a cross or a tick.

  • Signature, drawn or scanned

    Draw with your finger across the whole screen, or scan your signature from paper. The paper and shadows are removed automatically.

  • Real form fields

    If the PDF has fillable fields, you fill them in directly. Otherwise, tap where the text should go.

  • Password-protected PDFs

    Enter the password and fill in as usual.

  • Undo, redo and drafts

    Everything you add is kept as you work. It doesn’t disappear if you rotate the phone or close the app.

  • Preview before saving

    See exactly how the new PDF will look, and go back if something needs fixing.

  • The original is never changed

    Penlet always saves a new PDF. The file you received stays untouched.

  • Scan documents

    Photograph one or more sheets with Google’s document scanner. Edges are found automatically, and the pages become one PDF.

  • Share anywhere

    Send to email, messages or Drive from Android’s usual share menu. No extra sign-in.

  • English and Norwegian

    The whole app is available in English and Norwegian.

What people ask.

How do I sign a PDF on my phone?

Open the PDF in Penlet, tap “Signature” and draw your signature with your finger. Drag it into place, make it bigger or smaller, and save.

You can also scan your signature from a sheet of paper. It’s saved on your phone, so next time it takes one tap.

Can I fill in a PDF form on my phone?

Yes. If the form has fillable fields, tap a field and type. If it doesn’t, tap where the text should go. With “My details” you add your name, address and phone with one tap.

How do I scan a document to PDF?

Tap “Scan document” on the start screen and photograph the pages. Edges are found automatically, and several pages become one PDF you can fill in, sign and share.

Is a drawn signature valid?

Penlet places the signature you draw into the PDF. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature. Many recipients accept a drawn signature, but some documents require a dedicated e-signing service. Ask the recipient if you’re unsure.

Do I need an account?

No. Penlet has no account and no sign-in. Open the app and start.

Is the original file changed?

No. Penlet always saves a new PDF, for example “contract (signed).pdf”. The original stays untouched.

Is Penlet available for iPhone?

Not yet. Penlet is for Android. We plan an iPhone version later.
