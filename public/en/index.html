What people ask.

How do I sign a PDF on my phone?

Open the PDF in Penlet, tap “Sign” and draw your signature with your finger. Drag it into place, make it bigger or smaller, and save.

You can also take a picture of your signature on a sheet of paper – Penlet cuts it out and removes the paper. Your signature is kept on your phone, so next time it takes one tap.

My form has no fields to tap. Can I still fill it in?

Yes. Penlet finds the boxes and lines in a printed form by itself, so you just tap them and type. It works on forms you have photographed too. If it misses a field, tap where the text should go and type.

Can I turn a sheet of paper into a PDF?

Yes. Tap “Take a picture of a document” and photograph the sheets, several pages in one go. Penlet finds the sheet, straightens it, cleans up the background and makes a PDF you can search, fill in, sign and share.

Is a drawn signature valid?

Penlet places the signature you draw into the PDF. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature. Many recipients accept a drawn signature, but some documents require a dedicated e-signing service. Ask the recipient if you’re unsure.

Do I need an account?

No. Penlet has no account and no sign-in. Open the app and start.

Does the app need internet access?

No. The app does not even request internet access, and your documents never leave your phone.

The one exception is text recognition in photographed documents: it runs on the phone inside Google Play services, but the first time Google may download the language model.

Is the original file changed?

No. Penlet always saves a new copy, for example “contract (Penlet).pdf”, in the folder you choose. The original stays untouched.

What does Penlet cost?

You can try everything for free and save or share two documents. After that you unlock the app with a single purchase in Google Play, and it is yours for good. No subscription and no ads.

Is Penlet available for iPhone?

Not yet. Penlet comes to Android first. An iPhone version is in the works.
