How do I sign a PDF on my phone?

Open the PDF in Penlet, tap “Signature” and tap where the signature should go. The first time, you draw your signature with your finger or take a photo of it on a sheet of paper. Then you drag it into place, adjust the size and tap “Save”. You get a new copy of the PDF with your signature, and the original is not changed.

Step by step

  1. Open the PDF. Tap “Open PDF file” in Penlet, or tap the attachment in your email and choose Penlet under “Open with” or “Share”.
  2. Tap “Signature” in the toolbar at the bottom, and tap where the signature should go. If the form has a signature field, tap the field itself (“Sign here”).
  3. Make your signature. Draw it with your finger across the whole screen, or choose “Take photo” and photograph your signature on a sheet of white paper. Penlet finds the signature, you adjust the box around it, and the paper is removed.
  4. Drag it into place and make it bigger or smaller. If you slip, tap “Undo”.
  5. Tap “Save”. Penlet asks which folder the copy should go in and saves it as “name (Penlet).pdf”. Tap “Share” to send it straight to email, messages or another app.

Next time it takes one tap

Your signature is kept on your phone, so next time you choose “Use this one” instead of drawing it again. It is stored only on this phone and is never sent anywhere.

A signature from paper

Many people prefer their real handwriting. Sign with a pen on a sheet of white paper, hold the camera straight above and close, and take the picture. Penlet cuts out the ink and removes the paper, so the signature sits cleanly on the document. You can make it blue, black or keep it as on paper.

Is a drawn signature valid?

Penlet places the signature you draw or photograph into the PDF. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature. Many recipients accept a drawn signature, for example on contracts, consent forms and powers of attorney, but some documents require a dedicated e-signing service. Ask the recipient if you’re unsure.

Questions and answers

Can I use my signature from paper?

Yes. Choose “Take photo” in the signature window and photograph your signature on a sheet of white paper. Penlet finds the signature, you adjust the box around it, and the paper is removed.

Is a drawn signature valid?

Penlet places the signature you draw into the PDF. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature. Many recipients accept a drawn signature, but some documents require a dedicated e-signing service. Ask the recipient if you’re unsure.

Is the PDF or my signature uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your phone. Penlet has no account, and the app does not even request internet access.
